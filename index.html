Chapter 01

The Village

Where every story in this proposal begins.

Every morning, the children of our village walk to school past the same fields their grandparents knew. They carry notebooks, dreams, and the quiet hope that learning will open a door somewhere far away.

Their mothers carry something else: generations of craft in their hands — weaving, embroidery, and crochet passed down like an heirloom, practised quietly at home between all the work a household demands.

Yet in rural Pakistan, fewer than one in five women earn any income of their own. The talent is real. The demand exists — on the other side of the world. What is missing is the bridge between the two.

This proposal is that bridge. And like everything worth making, it is made one stitch at a time.
